Location: trading_engine.py / main.py The Issue: Your Strategy (4H) and Execution (1m/10m) are running in the same loop.

The Math: A 4-Hour candle is "Open" for 240 minutes.

The Bug: If a "Vol Expansion" breakout happens at Minute 1, the condition close > keltner remains TRUE for the next 239 minutes.

The Result: Your bot sees a valid signal every single minute. The cooldown (5 mins) stops it briefly, but as soon as the cooldown expires, it fires again on the same candle. You are buying the same breakout 20 times, paying fees 20 times, and getting stopped out on small wicks.

3. The "Taker Fee" Bleed
Location: bybit_broker.py (or order_manager.py) The Issue: Your logs show Slip:$3.39.

You are sending MARKET orders for everything.

Cost: 0.055% (Taker Fee) + 0.05% (Slippage) = 0.1% per trade.

Impact: On a round trip (Entry + Exit), you lose 0.2% immediately. If your target is 1.5%, you are giving up 13% of your profit to the exchange on every win.

The Fix: use LIMIT ORDERS for Entries and Take Profits. Only use Market Orders for Stop Losses.

4. The "Bootstrap" Inertia
Location: main.py -> bootstrap_system() The Issue: The bot calculates "Mike1" based on a 50-trade history.

Scenario: The market crashes today.

Lag: The bot has 49 trades from the "Bull Market" in its history. It will take ~10 losses in the Crash before the average drops enough to switch strategies.

Result: You lose money during every regime change because the bot is "slow to react."

The Fix: Regime Veto. Regardless of the Mike1 score, if the Instant Volatility (last 4 hours) is 300% higher than the Average Volatility (last 7 days), force the bot into Mean Reversion (or Cash). Do not let "Trend" run in a choppy volatility spike.

def check_regime_override(self, symbol):
    candles = self.data.get_candles(symbol, '4h')
    
    # Calculate ATR or StdDev
    current_vol = candles['close'].pct_change().std() # Last 24h
    historical_vol = candles['close'].pct_change().rolling(50).std().mean()
    
    # VETO POWER
    if current_vol > (historical_vol * 3.0):
        print("☢️ VOLATILITY SPIKE DETECTED (3x Normal). Forcing MEAN REVERSION.")
        return "MeanReversion"
        
    return None
